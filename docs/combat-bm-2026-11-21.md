Read alongside the accepted [passive schedule](combat-passives-2026-11-21.md) and [pet progression and persistence policy](combat-pets-2026-11-21.md). Later scoped addenda supersede earlier open questions only where they explicitly settle them.

## Accepted change

Use Multi-Shot / Мультивыстрел as the sixth Beast Mastery base ability at the proposed late-game slot, level 54. It replaces the Hunter's Mark candidate, giving this kit an owner-operated multi-target attack.

Hunter's Mark is not retained as an additional seventh base ability. Rapid Fire was considered but not selected for this Beast Mastery slot. Do not change Marksmanship's kit because of this decision.

## Reconciled dependencies

The passive addendum accepts Pet Frenzy at the owner's own level 15 for Beast Mastery; it does not select an additional level-30 passive. This settles the earlier blanket statement that passive unlock levels still needed review. It does not prove implementation, approve numerical parameters, or remove the requirement that Frenzy use eligible pet criticals rather than owner criticals.

The pet addendum defines hunter family unlocks at own levels 10/20/30/40 and per-pet state persistence across out-of-combat selection. Those are separate from the owner active-ability schedule above. Do not grant every family at 10, add taming or feeding, or treat switching as healing, resurrection or a cooldown reset. Detailed recovery and individual action rules remain subject to their own decisions.

On November 24 the user requested three browsable talent trees with point allocation across trees rather than an exclusive specialization-selection button. That is a separate redesign requirement, not implemented or fully specified by this historical kit record. Do not infer that the most-invested tree automatically grants or replaces this entire base kit, changes AI or activates an aura. Reconcile those dependencies explicitly before implementing mixed builds.

## Implementation boundaries and verification

Reuse the existing Multi-Shot definition where appropriate, but explicitly integrate it into Beast Mastery AI; a registry entry alone does not establish runtime use. Verify physical event typing, resource and cooldown checks, multi-target selection, aura interactions, and absence of unintended pet or passive proc duplication.

Do not add an extra speed buff or new ability to compensate for this choice. Numerical balance, AI thresholds, icon/sound verification, source integration and publication remain separate work. Accepted passive unlock design does not establish its execution or acceptance.

Record implementation revision and actual test results separately. No tests were executed for this documentation reconciliation; no gameplay code or publication is changed.
