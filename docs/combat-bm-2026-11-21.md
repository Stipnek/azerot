# Beast Mastery kit decision — 2026-11-21

## Status

User-approved design addendum to [Combat design decisions](combat-decisions-2026-11-21.md). Documentation only: no gameplay implementation, tests, or publication is claimed or authorized by this commit.

## Accepted change

Use Multi-Shot / Мультивыстрел as the sixth Beast Mastery base ability at the proposed late-game slot, level 54. It replaces the Hunter's Mark candidate, giving this kit an owner-operated multi-target attack.

Hunter's Mark is not retained as an additional seventh base ability. Rapid Fire was considered but not selected for this Beast Mastery slot. Do not change Marksmanship's kit because of this decision.

## Consolidated target kit

| Unlock | Abilities |
| --- | --- |
| Specialization selection, 15 | Arcane Shot; Scorpid Sting; Intimidation |
| 22 | Mend Pet |
| 40 | Bestial Wrath |
| 54 | Multi-Shot |

The chosen replacement and late slot are accepted here. Other schedule entries carry forward the review candidate; this addendum does not independently finalize every earlier unlock level or numerical effect.

Pet selection, pet actions, Frenzy, and other passive/role features remain separate from these six actives. The main character's future seventh talent ability remains unselected.

## Implementation boundaries

Reuse the existing Multi-Shot definition where appropriate, but explicitly integrate it into Beast Mastery AI; a registry entry alone does not establish runtime use. Verify physical event typing, resource and cooldown checks, multi-target selection, aura interactions, and absence of unintended pet or passive proc duplication.

Do not add an extra speed buff or new ability to compensate for this choice. Numerical balance, AI thresholds, icon/sound verification, passive unlock levels, and publication still require their respective review and tests.
